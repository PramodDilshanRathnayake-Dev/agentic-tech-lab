# MISSION STATEMENT
**Iteration:** v1.0

## The Objective
To build a fully autonomous, in-house **AI Agentic Orchestra** that serves as a next-generation software company management tool. 

## The Vision
This system must be distinctly "CEO-friendly," providing high-level visibility and Human-in-the-Loop control at critical audit checkpoints while abstracting away the underlying complexity of the software development lifecycle. By utilizing a shared mission-context, the agents (@pm, @architect, @uiux, @dev, @qa) will collaborate seamlessly to deliver enterprise-grade software.

## Key Directives
1. **Cost Efficiency**: Prioritize token-cost optimization over traditional middle-man overhead. 
2. **Scalability**: Support multiple projects per client out of the box.
3. **Resilience**: Ensure cloud cost self-healing mechanisms are deeply integrated.
