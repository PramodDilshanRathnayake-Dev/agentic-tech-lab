# Mission Statement - Iteration v1.2

## Mission: "Quantified Progress & Portable Execution"

### Objective
Our mission for this iteration is to provide transparency and reliability. We aim to empower the "CEO" with quantified insights into the project's health while simultaneously hardening our deployment infrastructure through containerization.

### Core Values
1.  **Transparency**: Metrics (Completion %, Sprints) should be accurate and instantly accessible.
2.  **Portability**: "It works on my machine" is replaced by "It works in Docker."
3.  **Efficiency**: Minimal friction for developers and manual reviewers.

### Key Results
*   **KR1**: Interactive health widgets on the Dashboard (Progress Bars, Timeline).
*   **KR2**: Successful local deployment of frontend and backend via Docker Compose.
*   **KR3**: CEO review of health metrics approved.
